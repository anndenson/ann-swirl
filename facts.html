Fun Facts about Ice Cream!

Note, these facts are from MyMochi. Click here to view the full list!
  1. It takes 12 pounds of milk to produce only one gallon of ice cream!
  2. Did you know that chocolate ice cream was discovered before vanilla?
  3. Some of the strangest flavors in the world are avocado, chili, garlic, and bacon!
  4. The average American eats a whopping 45.8 pints of ice cream in a year!
  5. The tallest recorded ice cream cone was over 9 feet tall in Italy!
  6. The first written ice cream recipe was found in a 1665 recipe book!
